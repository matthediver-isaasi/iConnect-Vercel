On this member record:

`https://bnms.dev.iconn.app/members/646fd80b-ecdf-439d-809c-10ef2cc8a5d6?tab=membership`

the member has paid for their membership via **GoCardless**.

The membership currently shows a **Current Term End** date and a **12-month billing period**. I want to confirm that the existence of a billing period does **not automatically mean that GoCardless collections stop at the end of that period**, as that is not necessarily the intended behaviour.

We need the Direct Debit setup on each membership structure to explicitly define both:

* whether collections stop or continue at the end of the billing period; and
* whether the monthly collection amount remains fixed or follows the currently active membership structure pricing.

This should give us four possible behaviours:

### A. Fixed amount, collections stop at term end

* Stamp the monthly amount when the membership starts.
* Continue collecting that same agreed monthly amount throughout the billing period.
* Ignore subsequent membership structure price changes for that term.
* Stop collections when the billing period ends.
* The member must then renew before further collections can take place.

### B. Dynamic amount, collections stop at term end

* Collections run only until the end of the billing period.
* At each monthly collection, consult the membership structure that is active at that point.
* Use the current monthly price from that structure.
* Any pricing change to the active membership structure therefore intentionally affects subsequent collections.
* Stop collections when the billing period ends.

### C. Fixed amount per term, collections continue

* Stamp the monthly amount when the membership starts.
* Use that fixed amount for the duration of the current billing period.
* At the end of the billing period, do **not** stop the Direct Debit collections.
* Start a new membership term and restamp the monthly amount using the membership structure/pricing active at that point.
* Continue using the newly stamped amount for the next billing period.

This effectively allows pricing to change at renewal, but not part-way through a membership term.

### D. Dynamic amount, collections continue

* Collections continue beyond the end of the billing period.
* The billing period still defines the membership term/renewal cycle, but it does not terminate the Direct Debit.
* At every monthly collection, consult the membership structure that is active at the time of collection.
* Collect the monthly price currently defined by that structure.
* Therefore, a price change to the active membership structure can intentionally change the member's next collection amount.

### Admin configuration

Please add the necessary configuration to the **Direct Debit / GoCardless setup on the membership structure** so that the administrator can explicitly choose which of these four behaviours applies.

Ideally, do not present this simply as four opaque options if the existing UI supports a clearer approach. It may be better represented as two separate settings:

**At the end of the billing period**

* Stop collections.
* Continue collections.

**Monthly collection amount**

* Fixed/stamped for the membership term.
* Use the current active membership structure price.

The combination of those two settings produces the four behaviours above.

### Member record

Whatever behaviour has been configured must also be clearly and accurately shown on the member's **Current Membership Commitment** section.

For example, the member/admin view should make it obvious:

* Current billing period.
* Current term end.
* Whether collections stop or continue at the end of the term.
* Whether the monthly amount is fixed for the term or dynamically follows the active membership structure.
* Current monthly collection amount.
* Where applicable, whether the amount will be restamped at the next renewal.

The important distinction is that **membership term end and Direct Debit collection end are not necessarily the same thing**. The system must treat them separately and report the configured behaviour clearly.
