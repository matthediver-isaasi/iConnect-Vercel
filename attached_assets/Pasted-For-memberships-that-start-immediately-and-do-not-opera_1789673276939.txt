For memberships that start immediately and do not operate on a fixed annual renewal cycle, we still need to calculate and store a **Membership Renewal Date**.

The renewal date should be calculated from the membership start date using the **Billing Period** defined on the Pricing tab of the membership structure that was selected when the membership began.

There are three main payment scenarios:

### 1. Membership paid in full

Where the member pays the entire billing period upfront, calculate the Membership Renewal Date using:

**Membership Start Date + Billing Period**

For example, if the membership starts on 15 September 2026 and the Billing Period is 12 months, the renewal date should be 15 September 2027.

This renewal date is required so that iConnect can trigger the appropriate membership renewal flows before the current billing period expires.

### 2. Membership paid monthly via Stripe

Where the membership is paid by recurring monthly Stripe payments, the member is still joining for the defined **Billing Period**.

The Membership Renewal Date should therefore also be calculated as:

**Membership Start Date + Billing Period**

The Stripe subscription/payment schedule should correspond to that billing period.

At the end of the member's current billing period, the membership must enter the normal renewal process rather than simply treating the existing payment arrangement as an indefinite membership.

The renewal should then use the membership structure and pricing that are valid at the point the new billing period begins.

### 3. Membership paid monthly via GoCardless

GoCardless collections can potentially continue indefinitely, so iConnect must control the membership billing period independently of the GoCardless mandate or recurring collection mechanism.

Again, calculate:

**Membership Start Date + Billing Period**

The critical rule is that the pricing agreed at the start of the member's current billing period must remain fixed for that entire billing period.

Membership structures can have an **Effective From** date, meaning a new membership structure or new pricing may become valid while an existing member is part-way through their current billing period.

This must **not** change the member's existing GoCardless collections during that billing period.

For example:

* Member starts: 15 September 2026
* Billing Period: 12 months
* Monthly fee agreed at start: £20
* Renewal date: 15 September 2027
* A new membership structure becomes effective: 1 January 2027
* New monthly fee: £25

The member must continue to be charged **£20 per month until 14 September 2027**.

At renewal on 15 September 2027, iConnect should determine which membership structure is effective on that date. If the £25 structure is then the valid structure, the member's renewed billing period should use the new £25 rate.

### Core rule

A member's current billing period must effectively be treated as a pricing commitment.

Changes to membership structures or pricing during that billing period must not retrospectively change:

* the agreed membership price;
* monthly Stripe payments;
* monthly GoCardless collections; or
* the current billing period end/renewal date.

Only when the member reaches their **Membership Renewal Date** should iConnect evaluate the membership structures available at that time and determine the pricing and conditions for the member's next billing period.

In practical terms, iConnect therefore needs to retain enough information against the member's active membership to know:

* Membership Start Date
* Membership Renewal Date
* Billing Period used at commencement/last renewal
* Membership structure/pricing version used for the current billing period
* Agreed price for the current billing period
* Payment frequency/method

The member should remain attached to that pricing commitment until the renewal process creates the next billing period.
