Can you first confirm my understanding of the current member-facing functionality?

At present, I believe a standard member has no way to view the details of their own membership setup or membership transactions.

The tenant-admin membership view is available here:

`/members/9d71ce1f-0fff-4c0a-91c7-f59efc3bcae4?tab=membership`

However, this appears to be RBAC-gated to tenant administrators and is therefore not available to the member themselves.

Members do have access to `/History`, but this currently appears to show event-related transactions only. There is also a **Membership** tab within `/History`.

Please investigate and confirm:

* What the Membership tab on `/History` is currently intended to display.
* Whether any membership transactions, payments, invoices or membership records are currently configured to appear there.
* Whether standard members currently have any other route where they can view their own membership details, renewal dates, payment history or invoices.

If my understanding is correct and the Membership tab is currently unused or incomplete, we should use this area as the member-facing membership account view.

The intention would be for the **Membership** tab within `/History` to provide the member with a similar view of their own membership information to that available to a tenant administrator, but restricted entirely to the logged-in member's own records.

This should include, where available:

* Current membership type/class.
* Membership status.
* Membership start date.
* Renewal/expiry date.
* Billing period.
* Payment method.
* Membership transaction history.
* Payment status.
* Membership invoices.
* Links to view or download invoices where supported.
* Any relevant renewal information.

The member should only ever be able to see their own membership and financial records.

Please first report back on the existing implementation and purpose of the `/History` Membership tab before making changes. If it is suitable for this purpose, then propose how we can extend it to provide the member-facing equivalent of the existing tenant-admin membership view without duplicating unnecessary functionality.
