At Aberystwyth University, we’re taking a different approach. Through co-creation, we’re forming opportunities that make a real difference to students and graduates alike.","<p>Graduates bring credibility, networks, and practical insight that students value. Yet, in many universities, careers and alumni activity rarely overlap, which can lead to missed opportunities. At Aberystwyth, the Careers and Employability Service (CES) and the Development and Alumni Relations Office (DARO) have joined forces to embed alumni into curriculum-based and co-curricular activities, strengthening employability outcomes and building lifelong connections.</p><p>This partnership builds on years of collaboration in student enterprise, where alumni donations fund prizes for the Start-up Competition and alumni themselves sit on judging panels and mentor winners - helping students turn ideas into viable businesses.</p><p>Following a joint Development Day between the two departments in August 2025, CES and DARO agreed two priorities for this academic year: embedding alumni in the curriculum and strengthening alumni connectivity through mentoring.</p><h2>Embedding alumni in the curriculum</h2><p>Alumni have always played a part in student learning at Aberystwyth, but this year we’re putting a stronger emphasis on making their involvement more consistent and impactful. Following our Development Day, one of the key priorities agreed was to embed alumni voices more firmly into the curriculum, giving students the benefit of real-world perspectives alongside academic learning.</p><p>To support this, we’ve introduced the Alumni Employability Fund. Managed by CES and supported through generous alumni donations from DARO’s AberFund, the fund helps departments bring alumni back to Aberystwyth – a rural location that isn’t always easy to access – so they can share their experiences in ways that directly support employability.</p><p>Departments work closely with their faculty Curriculum Development Consultants to design activities that link academic learning with professional practice and align with institutional employability aims. Through the fund, we aim to ensure that every academic department can welcome at least two alumni speakers for career-focused activities during this academic year.</p><h2>Strengthening alumni connectivity through mentoring</h2><p>Our second priority is expanding the Professional Pathways Internship Programme to include alumni mentoring. This year, we’re scaling up significantly - from just five mentor-mentee pairs last year to fifty – so more students can draw on the experience of alumni who’ve walked the same path and turned it into a successful career.</p><p>The Professional Pathways Internship Programme supports fifty final-year undergraduate students, with limited or no work experience, the opportunity to gain insight into non-academic careers within the higher education sector. These paid internships take place within professional services at Aberystwyth University, offering hands-on experience in roles that keep the university running behind the scenes. As a developmental programme, students are supported throughout, encouraged to reflect on the skills they’re building and given access to a range of training and resources.</p><p>Adding alumni mentors brings an extra dimension to the programme. Each mentor will meet with their student three times during the internship, providing advice, encouragement, and a real-world perspective on career development. For students, this means building confidence and networks; for alumni, it’s an opportunity to give back, stay connected with the university, and share their expertise in a meaningful way.</p><p><span style=""color: windowtext;"">We’re also looking to create a positive cycle of engagement. By linking our two Customer Relationship Management systems and drawing on DARO’s expertise, we can track students who’ve taken part in previous versions of the programme and invite them back as mentors. This means those who once benefited from the scheme can now support others, strengthening connections and building continuity. </span></p><h2>Lasting benefits</h2><p>Collaboration between CES and DARO at Aberystwyth reflects the principles of the university’s <a href=""https://www.aber.ac.uk/en/about-us/strategic-plan/"" rel=""noopener noreferrer"" target=""_blank"">Strategy 2030s</a> plan. By working together, we grow and build communities that connect graduates and students in networks of mutual support. These partnerships enrich the student experience and strengthen the sense of belonging that defines Aberystwyth.</p><p>Continuing this work, and strengthening it where we can, means creating more opportunities for students and deeper relationships with alumni. With clear responsibilities and open communication, these partnerships deliver lasting benefits for individuals and for the university.
