`/admin/integrations`

`member_class`

Use this spreadsheet to resolve those unmatched records and complete their membership setup.

**1st of each month**

**1 January 2026 onwards**

* 1 February 2026

Each historical monthly membership payment entry must, wherever possible, be associated with the corresponding Xero invoice that was raised for that month's membership charge.

Use the existing Xero integration/data to identify the correct invoice and retain the relevant Xero invoice identifier/reference against the iConnect payment record.

The result should allow us to see a member's historical monthly membership charges in iConnect and trace each entry back to the corresponding Xero invoice.

### 6. Migration Safety

The process must be safe to rerun.

Before creating any membership, mandate relationship, payment history entry or Xero association, check whether the equivalent record already exists.

Running the migration more than once must **not** result in:

* duplicate memberships;
* duplicate GoCardless mandate associations;
* duplicate monthly payment/history entries;
* duplicate Xero invoice associations; or
* any accidental GoCardless collections.

Where a record cannot be confidently matched, flag it for review rather than making an assumption.

### Expected End State

Once complete, each applicable BNMS member should have:

* the correct membership based on `member_class`;
* their existing GoCardless mandate correctly associated;
* a regular collection day of the **1st of the month**;
* monthly membership history populated from **1 January 2026**;
