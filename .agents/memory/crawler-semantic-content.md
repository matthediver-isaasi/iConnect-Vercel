name: Crawler semantic content
description: Owner-approved purpose of the unstyled crawler response and its content boundary.
---

The owner accepts an unstyled crawler page for indexing, but it must retain full public content and semantic text tags, including the page-title heading and authored heading hierarchy.

**Why:** Search Console exposed a genuinely truncated article response, not merely a cropped screenshot. The owner explicitly distinguished unstyled HTML from flattened plain text.

**How to apply:** Keep short descriptions separate from full body content. Sanitize rich content without stripping its meaning or headings. Crawler responses remain guest-visible: this policy does not authorize exposing member-only content.
