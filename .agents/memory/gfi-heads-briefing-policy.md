name: GFI Heads briefing enrollment policy
description: One-time correction is not permission for ongoing role-driven enrollment.
---

GFI Heads briefing correction is one-time only. Future membership and role
changes must retain existing enrollment behavior; do not introduce automatic
subscriptions or change category-count semantics.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected automatic enrollment while authorizing
missing subscriptions for current eligible Head of Service members without opt-outs.

**How to apply:** Keep any repair separate from application hooks and schedules.
Preserve explicit negatives, global flags and historical unsubscribe records;
the similarly named Head of Service with AI role is not the approved role.
