---
name: Annual membership value semantics
description: Recorded net value is not settlement; structure allocation and lifecycle evidence boundaries.
---

Annual organisation membership value means saved net membership commitment value, not paid revenue. Allocate by the applied structure's effective start, not the term, invoice or payment date.

**Why:** The revised reporting requirement deliberately includes unpaid recorded commitments and does not reconcile refunds/credit notes. Payment setup can itself create history rows, so row existence alone also cannot prove finalisation.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction when adding reporting modes. Never substitute payment status as the finalisation gate, infer an annual period from membership-year text, or recompute historical prices from today's structure.
