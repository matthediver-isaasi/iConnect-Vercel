---
name: Project discussion policy
description: Approved conversation-preservation, notification and author-control boundaries for task discussions.
---

Deleting a task's thread starter must preserve the conversation by promoting the
earliest surviving reply, not by deleting the subtree or replacing it with a copy.
Replies are deliberately single-level.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested conversation preservation, including
original authorship, posting times, private image references and reactions.

**How to apply:** Future discussion changes must retain survivor identity and
references. Removing an image reference never authorizes deleting a task
attachment, shared object or cover.

Replies and reactions do not authorize new automatic email/subscription delivery.
Explicit mentions retain their existing delivery only; editing does not re-notify.
Administrators have no override to edit/delete another author's comment.

**Why:** The owner explicitly excluded both expanded notifications and
administrator comment overrides from the discussion feature.

**How to apply:** Treat broader moderation or subscription features as separate
product decisions requiring approval, not implied parity with forums/articles.
