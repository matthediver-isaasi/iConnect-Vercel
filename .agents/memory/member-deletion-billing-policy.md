---
name: Member deletion and recurring billing
description: Deletion is not cancellation authority; unresolved recurring billing blocks destructive disposal.
---

Member anonymisation must not be interpreted as authority to cancel a genuine subscription. Keep access removal, provider cancellation, and financial evidence retention separate.

**Why:** Removing a member's identity while collections continue leaves billing without a usable customer identity; automatically cancelling instead could terminate a valid agreement without permission.

**How to apply:** Require billing resolution before destructive disposal, including merge and organisation cascade paths. Cancellation remains an explicitly authorised billing action. A local billing preflight is not a live-provider reconciliation claim; partial or failed checks must remain visible and block disposal.
