---
name: Event sponsor policy
description: Event-owned sponsorship levels and optional organisation identity links.
---

Sponsorship level belongs to a sponsor's assignment to a specific event, not to the sponsor itself. The same sponsor may be Gold at one event and Bronze at another. An explicitly uncategorised assignment must not inherit the historical sponsor-wide category.

**Why:** The owner requested independent event categorisation and consistent event-specific labels on CMS pages and emails.

**How to apply:** Keep category edits isolated to the event. Preserve existing effective labels when migrating older records, including saved CMS category filters and order. Do not alter previously delivered emails.

Linking a sponsor to a tenant organisation is optional. Standalone sponsors remain supported; curated sponsor presentation details remain independent and linking does not overwrite the organisation.

**Why:** The owner approved adding organisation linkage while retaining existing standalone sponsor behaviour.

**How to apply:** Validate organisation links within the tenant; do not infer authority to publish additional organisation details or synchronise records automatically.
