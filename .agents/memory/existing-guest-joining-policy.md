---
name: Existing guest joining forms
description: Separate public joining from signed-in guests purchasing their first membership.
---

BNMS wants separate versions of its joining forms for existing guest accounts, named with the suffix “for existing member”.

**Why:** Guests already have member records, bookings and activity. Their first paid membership should use that identity rather than create a duplicate. The owner agreed to separate forms instead of a dual public/authenticated contract.

**How to apply:** Preserve the category-specific joining questions, pricing and approval process; do not substitute renewal workflows or add Full-member questions to Associate or Student forms. Signed-in prefills must not initialize the requested membership class from the guest's old class. Preserve existing login and consent preferences. Keep copies inactive until reviewed; changing questionnaire destinations or activating copies is a separate action.
