---
name: Event survey invitation policy
description: Attendance and confirmation requirements for individual survey invitations.
---

Per-registrant survey invitations require a currently open event survey.
Attendance-controlled events must not offer an override for unverified
attendees. Events without attendance controls require a prominent warning and
explicit acknowledgement before sending. Always offer an email preview.

**Why:** The owner requires invitations to respect actual event attendance,
while allowing deliberate invitations when no attendance verification was set
up. An absent provider snapshot does not prove tracking was never configured.

**How to apply:** Recheck server-side source policies, current attendance,
survey availability and attendee address at send time. Keep guest recipients
separate from booker identity and preserve existing survey access rules.
