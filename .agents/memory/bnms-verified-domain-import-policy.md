For the Supporter domains workbook supplied on 2026-11-20, the owner approved organisation-specific domains only, preserving existing domains.

**Why:** Public-provider domains such as Gmail and Yahoo would admit all addresses at those providers, not just the individual contacts in the workbook.

**How to apply:** Exclude public-provider domain rows, personal addresses on the exact-email sheet, and Serac mappings explicitly marked for review. These exclusions require fresh approval before any later import; this approval does not create exact-address exceptions or authorize automatic organisation matching.
