---
name: Project board ownership
description: Ownership continuity, creator authority and explicit approval for legacy repairs.
---
Every new project board must have an Owner immediately, regardless of whether
it comes from Projects, Member Groups, automatic group creation or Sales.
A surviving board must not lose its final Owner through demotion, removal,
re-parenting or concurrent membership changes. Deleting the entire board is
different and may remove its memberships.

**Why:** The owner requested closing the gap after finding a board whose sole
member was an Admin and therefore could not manage ownership.

**How to apply:** Treat ownership as distinct from Admin access. Bulk sharing
must not downgrade existing Owners, and membership authority must be checked
against the current role under the same serialization as the write. Preserve
normal handoff once another Owner exists and existing group qualification rules.

Do not automatically promote creators or sole Admins on historical ownerless
boards. Repairing one approved board is not authority to repair all boards.

**Why:** The user separately approved the specific legacy repair; creation
provenance alone does not establish present-day ownership authority.

**How to apply:** Ask for the intended recipient/scope before legacy privilege
changes, and keep those repairs separate from the general safeguards.
