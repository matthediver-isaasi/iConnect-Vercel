---
name: GoCardless monthly DD membership plans
description: Design rules for the monthly Direct Debit membership checkout, agreement snapshot, and portal/admin plan surfaces.
---

- The DD offer is derived from the membership SIMULATION result, never raw config: flat pricing reads the config monthly amount, banded pricing reads the matched band's amount and must NOT fall back to the config amount.
- **Why:** a banded tier with a config-level amount would silently charge the wrong price for a member whose band has no DD amount — absence of a band amount means "DD not offered for this band".
- Collection authority and pricing policy are separate consent-time decisions. Later structure settings never authorise continuation or variable pricing for an existing agreement; only explicit dynamic consent permits subsequent active prices.
- **Why:** the membership billing period describes a term, not the lifetime of a reusable mandate. Treating either today's configuration or a displayed duration as consent can produce unauthorised collections.
- **How to apply:** preserve evidenced legacy renewal booleans and fixed prices; absent evidence means review, not implicit stop or continuation. Keep arrears collection blockers separate from normal term-end policy.
- New monthly-membership GBP/Bacs Billing Requests are mandate-only. Fixed-price terms use finite subscriptions; variable-price terms use individually reserved mandate payments, never both. Legacy in-flight snapshots with `billing_request_payment` retain first-payment plus N−1 behavior.
- Do not send `subscription_request` or `instalment_schedule_request` for GBP/Bacs Billing Requests. GoCardless supports them only for ACH/PAD, and its sandbox rejects Bacs with `request_type_not_supported_by_scheme`.
- **Why:** the native GoCardless Bacs consent cannot display a finite subscription schedule; implementing the nominal recurring payload breaks every new UK Direct Debit setup before consent.
- **How to apply:** show the two policies, amount, currency, duration, and collection timing in the app before opening GoCardless, then create only the collection mechanism authorised by that snapshot.
- A mandate-only subscription start must be on/after both today and GoCardless's freshly fetched `next_possible_charge_date`; always refresh/persist it before creation, even when the mirror has a date.
- **Why:** anniversary-day schedules can otherwise select a date before Bacs permits collection, causing GoCardless to reject the complete finite plan after consent.
- Initial-payment finalization needs its own durable completion marker outside the immutable DD snapshot. Reconciliation may resume unfinished activation/accounting/completion, but must never let the old first payment clear arrears or retry state from a later collection.
- **Why:** provider payment, mandate, and fulfillment webhooks can arrive in any order or fail between local side effects; generic payment replay can otherwise either strand a paid membership or falsely recover later debt.
- Activation is rule-driven (`mandate` / `first_payment` / `manual`) via `decideMembershipActivation`; `mandate` also accepts a late first-payment trigger so out-of-order webhooks still activate.
- Renewal may reuse the mandate, but continuation authority must come from retained consent, never a new structure setting. Fixed continuation restamps only the successor term; it does not mutate the old term.
- Portal/admin surfaces are fed solely by `GET /api/membership/payment-plan` (member view + `?admin=1` RBAC-gated list); bank details are never exposed to the client.
- **How to apply:** any new DD surface or lifecycle path must read the agreement snapshot, not tier config, and go through the pure helpers in `api/_lib/gocardlessDirectDebit.js`. Keep first-payment reconciliation isolated from ordinary recurring-payment recovery.
- Form-originated recurring DD consent starts applicant-scoped and unbound; after the form pipeline resolves the member, one transaction must bind the agreement and create its pending membership-history row before any subscription is created.
- **Why:** Billing Request and mandate webhooks may arrive before the applicant exists as a member. Creating a subscription first can collect money for an agreement that no membership row owns, while retries can duplicate or cross-link the membership year.
- **How to apply:** distinguish recurring form DD from generic one-off form GoCardless with its own provider/state discriminator; browser confirm, Billing Request webhooks, mandate webhooks, and reconciliation must all finalize and reload the agreement before subscription creation.
- A provider may roll a requested non-working charge date forwards; rejecting its date only after creation is too late to prevent a charge outside the term.
- **Why:** GoCardless documents non-working-day roll-forward, while the mandate's freshly fetched earliest date is provider-authoritative. Calendar-month arithmetic alone cannot establish a bank-valid date.
- **How to apply:** reserve the intended cadence and a provider-valid requested date before submission, enforce the authorised boundary then, and keep provider evidence distinct from calculated prices in displays.
- Subscription-less terms cannot rely on a subscription-finished event to settle membership.
- **Why:** GoCardless emits individual payment events for variable collections, but no subscription lifecycle exists to close the term. A fully reserved schedule is not a fully paid term.
- **How to apply:** complete only from confirmed/paid-out evidence for every authorised collection, with atomic history/plan settlement and separately recoverable completion notification.
- Collection-day edits belong to the operational plan cadence, never the purchased consent snapshot. Fixed GoCardless subscription dates require cancel/recreate; do not fake an in-place date change or provide replacement without a recoverable duplicate-safe transition.
- **Why:** reservations are the durable fence spanning an external payment call and its ambiguous/local-failure outcomes. A database lock alone cannot protect the period after the transaction commits while the provider call is in flight.
- **How to apply:** serialize amendments, reservation creation, and provider attachment/replay on the same plan lock; refuse amendments while any reservation is unresolved. Revalidate preview version, original anchor and reservation count. Attachment must acquire the plan lock before reading the current anchor, including old webhook replays, or it can restore a superseded next collection date. Keep existing submitted payments unchanged and let the provider’s current notice date determine banking-day eligibility.
- Completion must resolve each collection number against immutable applied amendment boundaries, not compare every historical payment with the latest operational anchor. Numbers at or before a boundary's reserved count retain the previous cadence; later numbers use the latest applicable amendment. Validate the version/anchor chain and preserve every existing amount, mandate, currency, provider-status and term-boundary predicate. This applies equally to stop and continuation consent; continuation never rewrites a preceding term's evidence.
