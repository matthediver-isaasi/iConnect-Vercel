---
name: Sales net reporting scope
description: Net Sales reporting and separate converted totals versus preserved manual opportunity forecasts.
---
The Sales dashboard and pipeline must display net-of-tax values throughout,
including historical converted opportunities. Manual estimates are net estimates.
This is read-side reporting authority only, not permission to rewrite stored
opportunity values, issued documents, tax calculations, payments, other report
views, exports or organisation commercial summaries.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested net dashboard/pipeline amounts while
retaining those other financial contracts and historical records.

**How to apply:** Use authoritative proposal/sale snapshot evidence rather than
a VAT divisor. Missing or ambiguous net evidence must remain unavailable, never
fall back to gross or appear as a genuine zero. Choosing an estimate's owning
proposal when several are linked requires separate product authority; do not
silently choose a proposal or add independent alternative quotations together.

The Opportunities list separately shows **Converted** and **Forecasted value**.
Converted is the sum of all confirmed converted proposals, excluding VAT, not
the latest proposal, unconverted alternatives or the opportunity's saved amount.
Forecasted value is the manually entered Opportunity Details amount.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose net converted totals and preservation of the
manual forecast when proposals convert.

**How to apply:** Retain the confirmed version's net evidence across subsequent
amendments and archival. Show zero only when no conversions exist or a confirmed
total is genuinely zero; missing or incompatible evidence remains unavailable.
Future ordinary and ticket conversions must preserve both forecast amount and
currency while retaining their normal won-stage and commercial effects. Do not
reconstruct or overwrite historical forecasts. This separate list contract does
not change the dashboard/pipeline's existing valuation rules or other reports.
