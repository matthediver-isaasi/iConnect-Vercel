---
name: Group card expiry
description: Expired group assignments should not appear in group detail member cards.
---

Hide members whose group assignment has expired from the group's member cards, including the card count and search. Retain the assignment for historical and administrative purposes.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that expired members be hidden from the group view. An active member account does not make an expired group assignment current.

**How to apply:** Evaluate group-assignment expiry independently of global member status. A current assignment may still qualify a member who also has expired assignments; preserve the expired history.
