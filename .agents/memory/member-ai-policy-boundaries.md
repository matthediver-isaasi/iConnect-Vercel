---
name: Member AI response policy boundaries
description: Why response preferences do not govern structured reporting or no-evidence facts.
---

Keep tenant response preferences subordinate to platform evidence rules; do not feed them to the structured-report planner or executor.

**Why:** The tenant brief describes a knowledge assistant's voice, not authorization to admit personal records or change separately gated reporting semantics. A style instruction must not change counted populations or supplied numbers.

**How to apply:** Extend presentation after authorization, preserve hard token ceilings, and keep no-evidence responses deterministic rather than asking a model to improvise from tenant guidance. Browser fixtures and prompt assertions demonstrate wiring, not real-model behavioural guarantees or member access under an administrator session.
