name: Exhibition bundle sales
description: Owner's exhibition sales model and distinction from example size mappings.
---
The owner sells exhibition stands through Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum bundles by event. Their example has two stand sizes with five physical stands of each size.

**Why:** User guidance must explain bundle-based selling, not only standalone stand products.

**How to apply:** Explain shared physical inventory across packages. The owner has not specified which bundle gets which size; any Bronze/Silver-small and Gold/Platinum-large mapping is illustrative, not an approved configuration.

BNMS exhibitor tiers categorise the bundle itself, not its component products.

**Why:** Several products are reused across Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum packages. Reclassifying shared products to match one bundle would misclassify the others. The owner approved separate bundle assignments using the shared sales category list.

**How to apply:** Keep tier categorisation separate from component product categories and physical inventory. Title matching was approved for the BNMS setup, not as a runtime categorisation rule for other tenants.
