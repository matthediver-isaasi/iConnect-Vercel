---
name: Opportunity archive policy
description: Owner-approved archive-first deletion boundaries for Sales opportunities.
---
Opportunities are archived before permanent deletion, with restoration available. Linked proposals or commercial records block permanent deletion, even when those proposals are drafts.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected blocking deletion when linked, rather than deleting commercial history with an opportunity.

**How to apply:** Archive is pipeline housekeeping, not cancellation authority for invoices, payments, proposals or fulfilment. Preserve linked project boards. Reuse existing Sales management and opportunity owner/admin authority rather than introducing hard-coded tenant roles.
