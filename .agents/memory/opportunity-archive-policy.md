---
name: Opportunity archive policy
description: Owner-approved archive-first deletion boundaries for Sales opportunities and proposals.
---
Opportunities are archived before permanent deletion, with restoration available. Linked proposals or commercial records block permanent deletion, even when those proposals are drafts.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected blocking deletion when linked, rather than deleting commercial history with an opportunity.

**How to apply:** Archive is pipeline housekeeping, not cancellation authority for invoices, payments, proposals or fulfilment. Preserve linked project boards. Reuse existing Sales management and opportunity owner/admin authority rather than introducing hard-coded tenant roles.

Proposals also use archive-first deletion, but permanent deletion is limited to unused drafts: every version must be never-issued and there must be no delivery, signature, financial or inventory history. Other proposals remain archive-only.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved protected proposal deletion rather than removing issued or committed commercial history.

**How to apply:** Reuse proposal-management permissions. Archiving must not cancel commitments or release reservations. Keep archived proposals linked to their opportunities; only an eligible draft actually deleted ceases to block opportunity deletion.
