---
name: Attendee title policy
description: Product boundaries for mandatory event attendee job titles.
---

An event attendee's title is a booking snapshot, not permission to update their member profile. The requirement is opt-in per tenant and does not retroactively invalidate existing bookings.

**Why:** The owner explicitly limited the requirement to new event signup/booking, excluding general profile rules, profile updates, historical backfills, imports and transfer management.

**How to apply:** Preserve completed purchase replay. Resolve only the attendee's own title; never use the purchaser's title for someone else or expose private titles through public email lookup responses.

Program-ticket credit purchases are not attendee registrations. Do not apply attendee requirements merely because a shared payment endpoint serves both.

**Why:** Credit may be purchased before any attendees or event places are chosen. Also, a tenant can change requirements while a card payment is awaiting its browser return.

**How to apply:** Gate new event payment initiation, preserve completed-payment replay, and route title-policy rejections of captured payments through provider-verified compensation rather than returning a bare validation error.
