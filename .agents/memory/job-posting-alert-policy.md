---
name: Job posting alert policy
description: Tenant enablement, all job sources, shared consent and protected campaign category.
---

Job posting alerts include both directly posted jobs and imported Adzuna jobs.
The tenant controls whether this feature is enabled. Members with Job Board
RBAC access manage the same subscription from Job Board and the last entry in
About-me's Communication Preferences. The Job postings communications category
must be non-deletable, show subscribers and remain usable for campaign sending.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested all sources and one subscription across
these surfaces, rather than a second independent preference.

**How to apply:** Keep category consent authoritative for both alert delivery
and campaigns. Tenant disablement and lost Job Board access must suppress
delivery, not silently resubscribe members. Do not generate retroactive alerts
for pre-existing jobs or treat edits to a published job as new publications.
