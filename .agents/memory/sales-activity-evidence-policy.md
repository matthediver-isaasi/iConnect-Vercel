---
name: Sales activity evidence policy
description: Commercial milestone meaning and historical evidence boundaries for Sales activity.
---

Sales dashboard activity is a commercial milestone timeline, not a generic audit
feed. Stage moves, proposal decisions, successful deliveries and confirmed sales
have separate meanings. Use recorded event dates and outcomes; never reconstruct
missing events from current statuses or rewrite historical records to populate it.

**Why:** The owner requested useful historical commercial context without
maintenance noise, fabricated attribution or speculative reconstruction.

**How to apply:** Keep delivery success independent of the later proposal-status
update. Do not call staff cancellation customer rejection, or proposal conversion
a confirmed sale without commercial evidence. Preserve genuine repeat actions
while consolidating transaction mirrors. Missing identities must stay explicit
rather than defaulting to an owner, sender or recipient.
