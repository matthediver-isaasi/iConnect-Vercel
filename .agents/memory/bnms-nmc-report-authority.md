name: BNMS NMC report authority
description: Owner-approved journal report eligibility and classification boundaries.
---

Members linked to BNMS's own tenant organisation must be excluded from all
NMC membership report counts, including review counts.

**Why:** The owner explicitly stated that members of the BNMS tenant organisation
should not be included in any counts, including staff using external email domains.

**How to apply:** Identify the primary tenant organisation and use the member's
organisation relationship, not the member's email domain. The owner identified
the BNMS tenant by its bnms.org.uk domain.

Do not guess staff scope when the primary organisation is absent or ambiguous;
stop report generation rather than publishing counts with uncertain exclusions.
Staff must also stay out of exclusion-reason counters, not just report sheets
and review totals.

**Why:** The owner requires exclusion from all counts, and guessing from names,
email domains or creation order could suppress ordinary external members.

**How to apply:** Require unique tenant-scoped primary-organisation authority
before projecting the report; leave membership records unchanged.

BNMS NMC fulfilment is classified by the member-scoped `member_class`, never roles. Eligible NMC classes receive print; all otherwise eligible non-NMC classes go exclusively to Online-only, even if category sheets are empty.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed this allocation.

**How to apply:** Do not infer print entitlement from category, role or postal-address presence.

Normal new monthly first-collection waits should be reported separately from
membership evidence problems, but are not fulfilment entitlement.

**Why:** The owner requested a clear healthy waiting category without relabelling
manual approval, failed collections, malformed records or historical uncertainty.

**How to apply:** Require tenant-owned live agreement, plan and history evidence.
Keep pending members outside eligible totals until confirmed payment and
activation; test transitions with fixtures, without provider writes.

Imported Active status plus a legacy expiry date, without membership history, is not sufficient membership evidence: flag these records for review. Active Honorary records without an expiry or membership history are recognised non-expiring members.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the first evidence rule and approved the second after reviewing aggregate live data.

**How to apply:** Preserve separately reviewed expiry-only membership-history evidence; do not broaden the Honorary exception to other classes, or interpret these reporting choices as renewal or payment authority.

The owner separately confirmed paid status for the reviewed 72 Full/Full Junior upfront members with retained expiries in 2027, approving missing history establishment with unknown start dates, amounts and invoice links.

**Why:** The previous upfront backfill covered only expiries from 22 September to 31 December 2026, leaving this unexpired group outside its scope.

**How to apply:** Treat this as a bounded administrator attestation, not provider settlement or renewal authority. Do not force these records into the earlier 2025/2026 contract or extend the approval to the expired or DD-evidence cohorts.

The owner subsequently confirmed paid status for the separately reviewed single overseas NMC membership. This is individual approval, not blanket approval for all overseas memberships.

**Why:** Archived invoices supported the membership description but omitted settlement status and currency; the owner supplied paid-status authority.

**How to apply:** Use the persisted per-member approval audit to identify the authorised record. Preserve unknown commencement, amounts and currency; archived invoice existence alone must not authorise payment or renewal.

The owner also confirmed paid entitlement through the retained corrected expiries for the reviewed missing-history cohort, including its six DD-evidence members and already-expired terms.

**Why:** Date normalisation exposed missing histories rather than incorrect seeded dates. The owner explicitly approved all members in that reviewed cohort, not just the upfront or unexpired subset.

**How to apply:** Use the recorded cohort audit to bound the approval. Expired terms remain expired. DD evidence plus operator-paid confirmation is historical membership authority only—not provider settlement, mandate activation, collection consent or renewal-policy authority. Do not extend this approval to other missing-history members.

The owner separately authorised establishment of one expired individual's paid historical term while requesting identification—not bulk repair—of the remaining missing-evidence records.

**Why:** That individual was outside the corrected-date cohort; approval of the earlier cohort did not cover him. The wider report count includes Former-class records and is not a count of current paid members.

**How to apply:** Use the individual approval audit to bound the write. Retain the known expiry and unknown start/amounts, with no renewal or billing authority. Review the other records by class, imported status, expiry and DD evidence; do not automatically exclude Former records or mark the wider cohort paid.

Historical entitlement approval may explicitly exclude cash-payment authority.
The owner approved a retained-expiry case on that basis while leaving another
reviewed record unselected, and approved unresolved identities only with unknown
costs and no invoice links.

**Why:** Credit-settled invoices and duplicate/conflicting provider identities
must not become verified cash merely because the membership is recognized.

**How to apply:** Treat an unselected record as unapproved. Preserve unpaid
status for entitlement-only approval, distinguish operator-paid attestation
from verified cash, and retain unknown commencement. These are historical
record corrections, never renewal, collection or invoice-link authority.
