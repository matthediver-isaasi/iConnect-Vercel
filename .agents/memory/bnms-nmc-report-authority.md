BNMS NMC fulfilment is classified by the member-scoped `member_class`, never roles. Eligible NMC classes receive print; all otherwise eligible non-NMC classes go exclusively to Online-only, even if category sheets are empty.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed this allocation.

**How to apply:** Do not infer print entitlement from category, role or postal-address presence.

Imported Active status plus a legacy expiry date, without membership history, is not sufficient membership evidence: flag these records for review. Active Honorary records without an expiry or membership history are recognised non-expiring members.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the first evidence rule and approved the second after reviewing aggregate live data.

**How to apply:** Preserve separately reviewed expiry-only membership-history evidence; do not broaden the Honorary exception to other classes, or interpret these reporting choices as renewal or payment authority.

The owner separately confirmed paid status for the reviewed 72 Full/Full Junior upfront members with retained expiries in 2027, approving missing history establishment with unknown start dates, amounts and invoice links.

**Why:** The previous upfront backfill covered only expiries from 22 September to 31 December 2026, leaving this unexpired group outside its scope.

**How to apply:** Treat this as a bounded administrator attestation, not provider settlement or renewal authority. Do not force these records into the earlier 2025/2026 contract or extend the approval to the expired or DD-evidence cohorts.

The owner subsequently confirmed paid status for the separately reviewed single overseas NMC membership. This is individual approval, not blanket approval for all overseas memberships.

**Why:** Archived invoices supported the membership description but omitted settlement status and currency; the owner supplied paid-status authority.

**How to apply:** Use the persisted per-member approval audit to identify the authorised record. Preserve unknown commencement, amounts and currency; archived invoice existence alone must not authorise payment or renewal.

The owner also confirmed paid entitlement through the retained corrected expiries for the reviewed missing-history cohort, including its six DD-evidence members and already-expired terms.

**Why:** Date normalisation exposed missing histories rather than incorrect seeded dates. The owner explicitly approved all members in that reviewed cohort, not just the upfront or unexpired subset.

**How to apply:** Use the recorded cohort audit to bound the approval. Expired terms remain expired. DD evidence plus operator-paid confirmation is historical membership authority only—not provider settlement, mandate activation, collection consent or renewal-policy authority. Do not extend this approval to other missing-history members.
