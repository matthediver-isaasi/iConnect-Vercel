---
name: Administrative membership waiver policy
description: No charge is a single purchased-period waiver, not future renewal pricing.
---
No charge in tenant-admin Start / Renew waives only the selected purchased period, including supported positive-price structures. Retain the original calculated price and the operator's audited reason. Do not infer free membership from a missing historical payment method.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested administrative Invoice and No charge without expanding member payment choices, rewriting structure prices, or creating a zero-price future renewal commitment.

**How to apply:** Keep member-choice and direct self-service allowlists unchanged. Use the existing atomic Start authority and zero-due activation workflow. Verification must not create live financial records or send emails to the linked member without separate approval.

The owner also requires administrative membership creation to allow a past start date, because administrators may record a membership after it has begun.

**Why:** Administrative entry can happen later than the actual membership commencement. A restriction to today or future dates prevents recording the correct term.

**How to apply:** Treat commencement and payment as separate facts. Choosing an earlier start date is not evidence of an earlier payment and must not bypass existing-purchase overlap or payment-provider protections.
