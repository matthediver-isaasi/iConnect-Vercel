---
name: Variable Direct Debit quotation
description: Distinguishing variable monthly collection consent from fixed instalments during in-grace renewal.
---

Variable-price Direct Debit must not be rejected merely because its credit-allocation period count cannot fit between today's date and the existing renewal boundary. Fixed monthly plans still require all agreed collections to fit.

**Why:** In-grace renewals remain contiguous with the previous term. Treating the variable plan's allocation count as a fixed payment obligation wrongly blocks members renewing after their anniversary. Shortening that count or moving the membership dates to bypass the check changes financial or membership terms.

**How to apply:** Use the saved, validated Direct Debit policy consistently in quotation and consent. Check that the next eligible variable collection fits, including nominated-day or anniversary rules, while preserving allocation counts, remaining credit, provider date checks and renewal-boundary controls. Unknown policy must fail closed.
