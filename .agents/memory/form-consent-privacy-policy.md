---
name: Form consent privacy destination
description: Product boundary between form Privacy Settings links and branding privacy policy links.
---

The optional form consent destination is a URL entered in Form Settings, not the branding Privacy Policy URL.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed these are separate destinations. Do not infer one from the other or introduce consent checkboxes or recording changes when adjusting this link.

**How to apply:** Preserve the independent URL and allow a link without a consent message; form link changes are presentation only.
