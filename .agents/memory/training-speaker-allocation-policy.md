---
name: Training speaker allocation policy
description: Owner-approved boundary between training agenda appearances and historical event-wide speaker evidence.
---

Training-event speaker recognition belongs to each agenda appearance. Existing event-wide training assignments must remain an explicit manual-allocation backlog; never fan them onto all agenda items.

**Why:** The owner chose manual allocation and prohibited historical splitting, backfills, retrospective sends and reinterpretation of issued awards/certificates. A parent event may already have started while a later appearance is still future.

**How to apply:** Keep event-level summaries/audiences distinct from entitlement identity. New eligible lines may have independent settings, vouchers and certificates; shared badges retain one credential with separate entitlements. Do not infer attendee points, bookings or attendance from speaker recognition. Ordinary simple/complex event behavior stays separate. Live migrations, customer allocation/remediation and real provider sends require separate approval.

Speaker voucher origin means the parent event that earned the award, not the event where credit is redeemed or the training agenda description.

**Why:** The owner explicitly distinguishes award provenance from later spending and prohibits historical inference from voucher codes or descriptions.

**How to apply:** Enrich existing awards only through their actual ledger voucher links and trustworthy source snapshots. Missing provenance is not permission to backfill financial records or reinterpret issued awards.

Read-only voucher provenance compatibility does not authorize installing any
part of the coordinated agenda-speaker migration set.

**Why:** The owner explicitly separated legacy display repair from the approved
coordinated rollout; adding the missing snapshot column alone would bypass that boundary.

**How to apply:** Keep display compatibility independent of schema installation.
Report isolated fixture evidence separately from an authorized deployed-session check.

Rollout drain evidence must include every deployment sharing the database and
provider work already in flight; aged leases and zero active SQL queries alone
are not sufficient.

**Why:** Preview and production can share the live database, and a worker can
await an external send or upload after its SQL transaction has finished.

**How to apply:** Keep local control preparation separate from installation,
deployment and pause approval. Never expire unresolved operation records merely
to unblock a rollout; verify termination and external outcomes first.
