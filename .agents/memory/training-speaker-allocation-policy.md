---
name: Training speaker allocation policy
description: Owner-approved boundary between training agenda appearances and historical event-wide speaker evidence.
---

Training-event speaker recognition belongs to each agenda appearance. Existing event-wide training assignments must remain an explicit manual-allocation backlog; never fan them onto all agenda items.

**Why:** The owner chose manual allocation and prohibited historical splitting, backfills, retrospective sends and reinterpretation of issued awards/certificates. A parent event may already have started while a later appearance is still future.

**How to apply:** Keep event-level summaries/audiences distinct from entitlement identity. New eligible lines may have independent settings, vouchers and certificates; shared badges retain one credential with separate entitlements. Do not infer attendee points, bookings or attendance from speaker recognition. Ordinary simple/complex event behavior stays separate. Live migrations, customer allocation/remediation and real provider sends require separate approval.
