# Membership structure scheduling policy

Version activation dates are independent of the fixed membership-year cycle. Retiring a pricing version never authorizes changing purchased terms, prices, invoices, mandates or payment commitments.

**Why:** GFI explicitly required automatic structure handover while retaining its 1 August–31 July membership year and all existing financial commitments.

**How to apply:** Correct only version schedule boundaries. Never use a version's activation day as a replacement annual anchor or as authority to reprice saved purchases.

Preserve intermediate historical versions when repairing an overlap; do not assume that two visible active cards are adjacent versions.

**Why:** GFI's reported two-card overlap actually contained a correctly retired intermediate version. The owner approved ending the oldest version before that intermediate version, not extending it to the newest version's start.

**How to apply:** Verify the complete live timeline and clarify any mismatch with the reported replacement relationship before live correction. Historical timestamps alone do not prove which earlier action caused an overlap.

## BNMS approved renewal-date coverage

BNMS authorised continuous, non-overlapping schedule coverage from 01/01/2026, using approved current settings for previously uncovered periods while preserving existing historical versions and prices. This does not authorise rewriting members' saved terms or changing renewal/grace windows.

**Why:** A member can be within grace yet unable to renew because no schedule covers the day after their saved expiry. Backdating every current version indiscriminately introduces overlaps and leaves renewal selection ambiguous.

**How to apply:** Preserve anniversary-based successor dates. Fill only uncovered periods, keeping existing version boundaries intact; do not interpret this approval as pricing authority for dates before January 2026.
