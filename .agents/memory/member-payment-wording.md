---
name: Member payment wording
description: Plain-English payment copy must retain financial meaning without changing payment behaviour.
---
Member-facing membership payment wording should avoid internal billing identifiers, raw activation codes and administrative explanations.

Show the selected membership schedule name rather than the pricing-model label “Flat Rate” on renewal forms. Do not default mapped individual-renewal forms to offering a billing-contact setup invitation.

**Why:** The owner found the pricing-model label unhelpful and the billing-contact invitation unexpected on individual renewal.

**Why:** The owner requested a content-only simplification across tenants, preserving payment, credit, renewal and activation rules.

**How to apply:** Use authoritative paired dates, preserve VAT distinctions, and never invent a membership type. A saved payment method or historical mandate does not establish a reusable active arrangement. Only describe renewal continuation and variable fees when the recorded policy supports them. Keep administrative policy helpers separate from member-facing copy.
