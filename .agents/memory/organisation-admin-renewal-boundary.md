---
name: Organisation administrative invoicing policy
description: Organisation invoicing must remain separate from individual renewal grace enforcement.
---

Organisation administrative manual processing and Invoice Now must not inherit individual membership renewal opening/grace restrictions. Keep individual membership policy unchanged, including explicit zero-day grace.

**Why:** The user confirmed established organisation annual invoicing must retain its historical availability after individual membership grace periods were introduced. A previous-year organisation commitment is real evidence, not something to discard because payment status is null or the UI says Year 1.

**How to apply:** Scope the administrative organisation exception narrowly. Preserve term continuity, exact pricing/year identity, duplicate and recurring-plan reservations, approvals and tenant isolation. Do not generalize it to member payment, public fee links or automated renewal consumers without a separate requirement.
