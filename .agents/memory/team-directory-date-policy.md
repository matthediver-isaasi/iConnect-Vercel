---
name: Team directory date policy
description: Meaning of join dates and activity indicators in the member-facing team directory.
---
Date joined means the member record's original creation/join timestamp, not a role-effective date or membership renewal. Do not invent or backfill missing historical dates for this display.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested record-creation chronology without changing membership or role policy.

**How to apply:** Keep join-date display changes separate from membership dates. “Never logged in” reflects confirmed absence of recorded member activity, independently of login permission; malformed timestamps or missing response projections must not make that claim.
