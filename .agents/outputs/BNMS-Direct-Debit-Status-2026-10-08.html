What has been resolved?

The software issue holding the first 102 accounting requests has been corrected. All 130 records previously awaiting status checks have now been checked directly against GoCardless: 129 are confirmed and one failed. The 129 confirmed statuses have been saved in iConnect and their accounting work added to the queue.

Why are the 231 payments queued?

Xero has reached its daily allowance for requests and is temporarily refusing further requests. This delays the accounting work; it does not mean these confirmed payments failed. Their original payment and invoice details are saved for processing when Xero permits it.

What happens next?

The 231 requests become eligible for automatic processing from 2:14am UK time on Friday 9 October 2026, based on the reset time reported by Xero. This is a start time, not a promise that every invoice will be finished immediately. Completion depends on Xero accepting the requests and the remaining checks passing.

For the queued payments: no manual action is needed at present. Please do not create replacement invoices or request another collection solely because the invoice is delayed. No additional GoCardless collection instructions were sent during recovery.

Understanding the
failed payments

The three payments already marked “failed” in iConnect have the bank-reported reasons below. These are collection failures, not Xero invoice delays. GoCardless reports that it will not automatically retry these three payments.

1. £10.66 — mandate cancelled

PM01XW45YJ2FKBK4JFG55489VX72

Reported reason: the member cancelled the Direct Debit mandate at their bank before the payment could be collected.

Suggested action: contact the member to confirm whether the cancellation was intentional. If they wish to continue paying by Direct Debit, arrange a new mandate with their authorisation, or agree an alternative payment method. Do not assume the cancelled mandate can be reused.

2. £10.66 — “refer to payer”

PM01XW45RVKWDM3H4VKKERTPJ7QT

Reported reason: the member’s bank could not pay the Direct Debit. GoCardless explains that this usually means insufficient funds, but banks sometimes use this response for other reasons. Insufficient funds is therefore a possibility, not a confirmed fact.

Suggested action: contact the member discreetly and ask them to check with their bank. Agree how to settle the outstanding payment. Before any retry, check the current mandate and payment status and follow the applicable authorisation and notification requirements.

3. £13.00 — “refer to payer”

PM01XW45FSM0KCSYDE098GR7CR9S

Reported reason: the same bank response as payment 2. Insufficient funds is common, but the exact cause has not been established.

Suggested action: ask the member to check with their bank and agree a payment arrangement. Do not describe insufficient funds as certain or assume an automatic retry will take place.
