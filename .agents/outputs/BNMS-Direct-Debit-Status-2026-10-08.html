Why are the 102 payments queued?

Xero has reached its daily allowance for requests and is temporarily refusing further requests. This delays the accounting work; it does not mean these 102 confirmed payments failed.

A separate software check had incorrectly held these requests for review. That issue has been corrected. All 102 were checked again, their original payment details were verified, and they were returned to the automatic retry queue.

What happens next?

The 102 requests become eligible for automatic processing from 2:14am UK time on Friday 9 October 2026, based on the reset time reported by Xero. Processing will run through the queue; this is a start time, not a promise that every invoice will be finished immediately.

Completion still depends on Xero accepting the requests and the remaining checks passing. The 130 records with an unconfirmed local status require separate follow-up and are not included in these 102 queued requests.

What should the administrator do?
No manual action is needed for the 102 queued requests at present. Please do not create replacement invoices or request another collection for them solely because the invoice is delayed. No additional GoCardless collection instructions were sent as part of this recovery.
